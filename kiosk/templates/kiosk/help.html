About Us

Product Information

What's the difference between Indica, Sativa, and Hybrid?
Indica: Generally provides relaxing, calming effects. Great for evening use and may help with sleep and pain relief.

Sativa: Typically offers energizing, uplifting effects. Often preferred for daytime use and creative activities.

Hybrid: Combines characteristics of both Indica and Sativa, offering balanced effects depending on the specific strain.
What do THC and CBD percentages mean?
THC (Tetrahydrocannabinol): The psychoactive compound that produces the "high" feeling. Higher percentages typically mean stronger effects.

CBD (Cannabidiol): Non-psychoactive compound known for potential therapeutic benefits like pain relief and relaxation without the high.
How should I choose the right product for me?
Consider your experience level, desired effects, and time of use. Beginners should start with lower THC products. Our knowledgeable staff are always available to provide personalized recommendations based on your needs.

Legal & Safety

What do I need to purchase cannabis?
You must be 21 years or older with a valid government-issued ID. We verify age for all customers, even if you've previously been verified.
Are your products tested for quality and safety?
Yes! All our products undergo rigorous lab testing for potency, pesticides, heavy metals, and other contaminants. Test results are available upon request.
What are the purchase limits?
New Jersey allows adults 21+ to purchase up to 1 ounce (28 grams) of flower or equivalent amounts of other cannabis products per day.

Storage & Usage

How should I store my cannabis products?
Store in a cool, dark place away from children and pets. Keep products in their original packaging to maintain freshness and comply with regulations.
